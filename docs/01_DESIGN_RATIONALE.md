## 1. Pedagogical Theories & Grounding

### Constructivism & Inquiry-Based Learning (Bybee 5E Model)
1. **Engage (Welcome):** Choose mode (Class Projector / Group Tablet), team avatar, and grade level.
2. **Explore (Ask & Guess ➔ Lab Bench):** Formulate hypothesis on light, warmth, or baking soda; run animated 10s simulation.
3. **Explain (Look & Think):** Compare results in data table and bar chart; reflect on surprises.
4. **Elaborate (Detective Hints):** 4-step scaffolding ladder prompts revision without giving away the answer.
5. **Evaluate (Check):** 5 multi-format assessment tasks with instant formative guidance and teacher-moderated feedback.

### Addressing Misconceptions
- **Misconception M1 ("Hotter is always better"):** Children assume more heat always accelerates growth. The simulation accurately models enzyme denaturation at 45°C where bubbles drop to 5/min.
- **Misconception M2 ("Plants make food in the dark"):** The simulation enforces the limiting factor principle: 0% light strictly produces 0 bubbles.
